# OpenStreetMap Full History

Historical versions of OpenStreetMap nodes, ways, and relations from the full-history planet dump. The planned tables contain object versions, tags, ordered way-node references, and ordered relation members.

The source PBF is dated 10 August 2026. Redacted versions absent from the public dump are not reconstructed. OpenStreetMap data is available under the Open Database License and requires attribution to OpenStreetMap contributors.
