# Open Food Facts Historical Snapshot

A dated product catalogue containing product identifiers, names, brands, categories, ingredients, allergens, nutrition values, labels, countries, packaging, and source timestamps.

One row represents one product state in the 16 August 2026 source snapshot. Nested objects are retained where flattening would discard information. Product images are excluded. Database records are distributed under the Open Database License; reused fields may have additional attribution requirements.
